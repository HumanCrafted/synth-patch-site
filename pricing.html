Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I hit 20 patches on the Free plan?

You won't be able to save additional patches, but you can always share patches publicly to earn bonus patches, delete some patches to make room, or upgrade to Pro for unlimited patches.

How do bonus patches work?

Free users can earn bonus patches by sharing publicly. You get +1 bonus patch for each public share, and +2 bonus patches if you include an audio clip. There's no limit to how many bonus patches you can earn!

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade to Pro at any time. If you downgrade from Pro to Free, you'll keep all your patches but won't be able to create new ones until you're under your patch limit (20 + any bonus patches you've earned).

What is Private Link sharing?

Pro users can create unique private links to their patches that anyone can view anonymously without an account. You can update or revoke these links anytime. It's perfect for sharing with subscribers, Patreon members, or collaborators. Or use them to create patch books you can share or sell on your own.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep Pro.
